About the journal

A closer look
at our tools.

hositaari is an independent technology journal for people who like to know how things work.

We write about the hardware on our desks, the software we keep, and the small design decisions that make a tool feel right. No release-day rush. Just careful observation, illustrated by hand-built code.

Our three shelves are simple: hardware, software, and design. Take a look around.
